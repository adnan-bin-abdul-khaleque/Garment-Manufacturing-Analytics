# 🏭 Factory, Buyer & Product Performance Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Business Intelligence dashboard** designed to provide a comprehensive view of a company's **financial performance, manufacturing operations, buyer/commercial performance, product performance, delivery efficiency, and quality metrics**.

The solution is structured into **four interconnected dashboard views**:

1. **Executive Summary** – High-level company-wide performance
2. **Factory & Production Performance** – Manufacturing efficiency, output, downtime, and quality
3. **Buyer & Commercial Performance** – Revenue, buyers, shipments, orders, cancellations, and regional performance
4. **Product & Quality Performance** – Product revenue, defects, yield, pricing, and category performance

The dashboards transform operational and commercial data into actionable insights that can help management identify performance gaps, monitor KPIs, and understand the major drivers of revenue, production, and quality.

---

# 🎯 Business Objectives

The primary objectives of this project were to:

- Monitor overall business and financial performance
- Track production output across factories and divisions
- Evaluate manufacturing efficiency and simplified OEE
- Analyze downtime and production utilization
- Monitor defect rates and product quality
- Evaluate buyer revenue contribution
- Analyze regional and country-level sales performance
- Track shipment performance and cancellations
- Compare product-category revenue
- Identify high-performing factories, buyers, and products
- Analyze production output relative to workforce and machinery
- Understand the relationship between product pricing, output, and defect rates
- Provide management with an executive-level view of business performance

# 🎯 Management Takeaways

From an executive perspective, the dashboard highlights several potential areas of focus:

1. **Improve shipment reliability**  
   With on-time shipment at **45.6%**, delivery performance represents a significant opportunity for improvement.

2. **Reduce operational downtime**  
   Downtime is **18.62% of available time**, suggesting potential gains through process and capacity optimization.

3. **Target quality improvement efforts**  
   The Pareto analysis shows that a relatively small number of product categories account for a large proportion of displayed defects.

4. **Protect high-value markets**  
   Europe contributes more than **$108M** in revenue and therefore represents a critical market.

5. **Improve productivity across factory types**  
   The difference between the highest and lowest displayed output-per-worker values indicates potential opportunities to identify and replicate best practices.

6. **Balance revenue growth with quality**  
   High-revenue categories should be monitored alongside their defect rates to ensure revenue growth does not come at the expense of product quality.
